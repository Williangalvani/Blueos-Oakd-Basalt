Camera link (XLink between the camera and the Pi)

–
stream (estimate)Mbpsmsgs/sper msg
Measured: depthai's XLink byte counters (device.getProfilingData), about once a second; they count message payloads, not the USB/Ethernet framing. Streams: what the Pi reads from each queue plus header estimates, over the last 10 s; the rest is protocol traffic and frames dropped in the Pi's queues. 100 Mbps is the raw Ethernet rate of a PoE camera; the usable rate is lower. ↑ streams go from the Pi to the camera.

Depth for PnP (depth_transport)

–
points: the camera computes the depth at each tracked feature (the average of the 5×5 window) and sends only those numbers; a frame whose points are missing or do not match its features gets no depth (counted here), never older depth. Images (and full depth frames) cross the link only while someone watches a live stream or runs the depth probe; "video idle" means nothing image-like is sent.

Image streams (what crosses the link to be viewed)

–
Images are JPEG-encoded on the camera. Raw frames encoded on the Pi are a fallback and are flagged here and in the widget. The depth snapshot is 16-bit millimetres (measurement data for the focal check and depth_raw.png), so it always crosses raw, one frame per request.
